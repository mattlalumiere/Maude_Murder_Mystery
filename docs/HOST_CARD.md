# Host Quick Card (one page)

Print this or keep it on your phone. About 15 minutes from start to playing.

## 10 minutes before guests arrive
1. Open the game site on a laptop. Tap **Host a game** and sign in.
2. **Set up the game:** party name, how many adults, kids, toddlers, length, spice, drinking. Prompts are optional; blank means the AI picks. Choose your AI picture budget (or pick "Text only").
3. Make up your **secret override code** (4+ characters). Do not say it aloud. Write it somewhere only you can see.
4. Tap **Create game**. You get a 4-letter **room code**.
5. Put the **big screen** page on the TV (the link is on your host page). A QR code appears.

## As guests arrive
- Send the **invite link** (copy button on your host page) or point phones at the TV's QR code.
- Each guest builds a character: name, age group, and (optional) backstory ideas. Kids who want to be the victim and come back as the Ghost tick the box.
- **No phone?** Use **Add player** (toddlers, grandparents) and hand their grown-up the link shown.
- Photos: adults only by default. Parents of kids can skip the photo; the kid still gets a cartoon character.

## Start
1. When 4+ players (2+ adults) are in, tap **Cast the characters and start**.
2. Everyone opens their card privately on their phone and taps "I've memorized it".
3. Read the house rules aloud: **Pass is always allowed. Drinks are optional (water or mocktail counts). Kids and toddlers play Snack or Silly. Everything is fiction.**

## During the game
- Use **Next** to move through the acts. The run sheet shows target times (about 2 hours).
- **Running late?** Skip a dare first. Then use your override code: Accelerate.
- **Pause** for naps, meltdowns, and refills.
- Call a **water round** every few drinks. Plan a designated driver.
- Anyone uncomfortable? Say "Pass", skip the dare, or turn off drinking and dares for everyone.

## Reveal
Tap **Next** into Act V. Scores and awards appear on the TV. Delete photos from the host console when you are done.

## If something goes wrong
| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Guest cannot join | Check the room code; joining closes when you press Start |
| Phone lost its card | Reopen the game page in the same browser (the card is remembered on that phone). If it still fails, use Add player before starting |
| Pictures stopped appearing | Keep your host tab open and awake; the game still plays without art |
| Override code forgotten | It cannot be recovered; play on without spoilers |
